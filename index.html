A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT ASK

Good questions.

How much will my website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the design and the features you need. Share your idea and I’ll put together a tailored quote. We agree on the scope and cost before the work starts.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. Send me the link and tell me what needs attention. I can review frontend issues, content updates, responsive layouts and performance improvements, then suggest a clear scope.

What do you need from me to get started?

A little about your business, your goals, any websites you like and your ideal launch date. If you already have a logo, text or images, those are useful too. We’ll work out the rest together.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Responsive layouts are part of the build, with attention to readability, navigation and interactions on mobile, tablet and desktop.
